TwinShot is a simple editor for making polished before and after comparison images. Upload two photos, style them, and export a ready-to-share image in seconds, all without leaving your browser.

- Side by side or stacked layouts with adjustable split
- Smart framing with fill or fit options and focal point controls
- Styled labels with custom text, fonts, colors, outlines and backgrounds
- Add your branding with logo placement in any corner
- Social-ready sizes including square, portrait, story and widescreen presets
- Reusable projects – save settings as a file and reuse on new photos
- Private by design – runs entirely in your browser with no uploads or tracking
- Open source under MIT license
- Works offline once loaded, no sign-up required
