Markly is an image annotation tool for people who need to point at something and explain it clearly — without uploading their screenshot anywhere.

- Instant to use — no install, no account, no naming step. Open the page, paste, and you're marking up within seconds
- Private by design — there is no server for your image to reach. Privacy isn't a promise in a policy — it's how Markly is built
- Focused toolset — arrows, boxes, ellipses, pen, highlighter, and text. The handful of tools that explain a screenshot, done well
- Paste, mark, copy, close workflow — take a screenshot, paste it, mark it up, copy the result, and go
- Nothing is uploaded — your image is opened and drawn entirely inside the browser tab
- No accounts — nothing to sign up for, so there's no profile tied to what you annotate
- No tracking — no analytics or third-party scripts watch what you do or see what you draw
- Nothing kept — close the tab and the image and marks are gone. Saving only happens when you choose to export
